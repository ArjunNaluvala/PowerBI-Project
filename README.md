# Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

# Overview

Customer retention is one of the biggest challenges faced by telecom companies. This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior using **Power BI** to identify high-risk customer groups, revenue leakage patterns, and service-level factors influencing customer attrition.

The dashboard was built using the **Telco Customer Churn Dataset** containing **7,043 customer records** and **21 business features**.

The project delivers executive-level business insights using:

* Interactive dashboards
* KPI cards
* DAX measures
* Decomposition Trees
* Advanced visualizations
* Dynamic slicers
* Business intelligence reporting

# Business Problem

Telecom companies lose recurring revenue when customers discontinue services.

The major business challenges addressed in this project:

* Identifying churn drivers
* Measuring revenue loss
* Understanding customer behavior
* Improving customer retention
* Predicting high-risk customers
* Supporting data-driven business decisions

# Objectives

* Analyze customer churn patterns
* Measure churn-related revenue loss
* Identify high-risk customer segments

# Key Business Insights

## Churn Drivers

* Month-to-month contracts had the highest churn rate (42.7%)
* Fiber optic users showed elevated churn behavior (41.9%)
* Electronic check users had the highest churn percentage (45.3%)

## Customer Retention Insights

* Churned customers averaged only 18 months tenure
* Retained customers averaged 37.6 months tenure
* Senior citizens churned at significantly higher rates

## Revenue Insights

* Monthly churn-related revenue loss exceeded $139K
* Long-term contracts significantly improved retention
* High-value customers required targeted retention strategies

# Business Recommendations

* Promote yearly contracts through loyalty discounts
* Improve customer support for fiber optic users
* Build retention campaigns for high-risk customers
* Encourage automatic payment methods
* Enhance onboarding experience for new customers
